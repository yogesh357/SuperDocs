# Task 3: Tell Us Where This Sells
This document analyzes ten high-value, commercial use cases for SuperDocs, specifying concrete pain points, target companies, key buyer personas, and business value.

---

### 1. AEC / Architecture: Contractor Substitution Verification
* **Use Case**: When contractors submit product substitution requests during construction, project architects must manually cross-reference candidate cutsheets against dense specifications (e.g., MasterFormat sections) line-by-line to verify compliance across parameters (dimensions, finishes, fire ratings, ASTM testing codes).
* **Target Companies**: 
  * Gensler
  * AECOM
  * HOK
  * Perkins&Will
  * Turner Construction
* **Key Buyer Persona**: Director of Specifications, Principal Architect, Head of Construction Administration.
* **Why They Buy**: Prevents expensive liability claims and delays resulting from installing non-compliant materials. Cuts evaluation time from hours to minutes.

---

### 2. Legal / Corporate Law: M&A Diligence & Redline Review
* **Use Case**: Junior associates manually compare hundreds of incoming acquisition documents, disclosure schedules, and seller-proposed contracts against the primary merger agreement to locate discrepancies in warranty claims, liability caps, and indemnification thresholds.
* **Target Companies**: 
  * Kirkland & Ellis LLP
  * Latham & Watkins LLP
  * Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom LLP
  * Baker McKenzie
* **Key Buyer Persona**: M&A Practice Group Leader, Head of Legal Operations.
* **Why They Buy**: Increases billing capacity and accuracy by automating routine document comparisons, reducing the risk of missing critical indemnification discrepancies during late-night reviews.

---

### 3. Medical Device / Biotech: FDA 510(k) Regulatory Auditing
* **Use Case**: Comparing regulatory submissions (such as 510(k) clearance documentation) against predicate devices line-by-line across mechanical tolerances, biomaterial specs, and ISO testing protocols to prove substantial equivalence.
* **Target Companies**: 
  * Medtronic
  * Pfizer Inc.
  * Johnson & Johnson
  * Boston Scientific Corporation
* **Key Buyer Persona**: VP of Regulatory Affairs, Director of Quality Assurance (QA).
* **Why They Buy**: Speeds up time-to-market. The FDA submission process is highly document-heavy; finding a single misaligned parameter early saves weeks of regulatory feedback loops.

---

### 4. Aerospace & Defense: Subcontractor Technical Spec Compliance
* **Use Case**: Verifying that thousands of subcontractor-supplied part drawings, metallurgical certifications, and assembly manuals fully comply with strict Department of Defense (DoD) military standards (MIL-STD) and aerospace specifications.
* **Target Companies**: 
  * Lockheed Martin Corporation
  * Northrop Grumman
  * Boeing Defense, Space & Security
  * General Dynamics
* **Key Buyer Persona**: Chief Quality Engineer, Director of Supply Chain Quality.
* **Why They Buy**: Reduces manufacturing defect rates and avoids heavy government non-compliance penalties or supply-chain standdowns.

---

### 5. Commercial Real Estate: Lease Abstracting & Due Diligence
* **Use Case**: Reviewing thousands of tenant leases during building acquisitions to extract and compare key variables (rent escalations, maintenance clauses, termination rights, occupancy limits) against a master underwriting model.
* **Target Companies**: 
  * CBRE Group, Inc.
  * JLL (Jones Lang LaSalle)
  * Cushman & Wakefield
  * Blackstone Group (Real Estate Division)
* **Key Buyer Persona**: VP of Acquisitions, Head of Asset Management, Director of Lease Administration.
* **Why They Buy**: Eliminates the high cost of outsourcing lease abstracting to third parties and guarantees no missed termination clauses or hidden liabilities in properties under contract.

---

### 6. Investment Banking: Loan Covenant Compliance Auditing
* **Use Case**: Auditing borrower financial reporting sheets, compliance certificates, and credit agreements periodically to ensure the business does not breach debt covenants (leverage ratios, minimum liquidity levels).
* **Target Companies**: 
  * JPMorgan Chase & Co.
  * Goldman Sachs Group, Inc.
  * Citigroup Inc.
  * Wells Fargo & Co.
* **Key Buyer Persona**: Head of Credit Risk Management, Chief Compliance Officer.
* **Why They Buy**: Acts as an early warning system for default risk, saving millions in write-downs by immediately highlighting covenant discrepancies.

---

### 7. Insurance / Underwriting: Commercial Policy Comparison
* **Use Case**: When large commercial entities request renewals or bid quotes, underwriters must compare custom policy coverage options against previous packages line-by-line to identify exclusions, limits, and deductible differences.
* **Target Companies**: 
  * State Farm Insurance
  * Liberty Mutual Group
  * Allstate
  * Chubb Limited
* **Key Buyer Persona**: Chief Underwriting Officer, Director of Commercial Lines.
* **Why They Buy**: Protects margins by ensuring underwriters do not accidentally inherit higher risk classes due to unspotted policy line edits.

---

### 8. Consulting / Enterprise IT: RFP Response Security Review
* **Use Case**: Comparing vendor Request for Proposal (RFP) response sheets against corporate information security (InfoSec) standards (SOC2 controls, GDPR compliance checklists) to flag vendor non-compliance.
* **Target Companies**: 
  * Accenture
  * Deloitte LLP
  * PwC (PricewaterhouseCoopers)
  * McKinsey & Company
* **Key Buyer Persona**: VP of Procurement, Head of Vendor Risk Management.
* **Why They Buy**: Speeds up procurement cycles by automatically highlighting which vendors fail security benchmarks, saving IT security teams days of manual review.

---

### 9. Energy & Utilities: Environmental Impact Report (EIR) Review
* **Use Case**: Cross-referencing multi-thousand-page Environmental Impact Reports (EIR) against municipal codes, EPA regulations, and project parameters to ensure zero compliance gaps prior to breaking ground.
* **Target Companies**: 
  * Duke Energy Corporation
  * NextEra Energy
  * Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E)
  * Chevron Corporation
* **Key Buyer Persona**: VP of Environmental Health and Safety (EHS), Director of Project Development.
* **Why They Buy**: Avoids costly lawsuits, environmental injunctions, and administrative fines that halt large infrastructure projects.

---

### 10. Franchise Operations: Operations Manual Compliance Audits
* **Use Case**: Periodically comparing individual franchise franchise agreements and locally adapted operating agreements against the master brand standards manual to enforce consistency in service delivery, logo rules, and ingredient sourcing.
* **Target Companies**: 
  * McDonald's Corporation
  * Starbucks Corporation
  * Subway IP LLC
  * Marriott International, Inc.
* **Key Buyer Persona**: VP of Franchise Operations, General Counsel.
* **Why They Buy**: Protects global brand equity and prevents litigation by identifying out-of-compliance franchise practices instantly.
