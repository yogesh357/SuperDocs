# Task 3: Tell Us Where This Sells

SuperDocs can be positioned as a document intelligence and editing layer for organizations whose workflows depend on comparing, updating, and maintaining consistency across large collections of business documents.

The following are ten high-value commercial opportunities where SuperDocs could reduce repetitive document work, improve consistency, and keep humans in control of important document changes.

---

## 1. AEC / Architecture — Contractor Substitution Verification

**Use Case:**
During construction, contractors submit product substitution requests containing product cut sheets and supporting documentation. Project architects and specification teams must compare the proposed product against the relevant specification section, checking requirements such as dimensions, finishes, fire ratings, performance standards, and testing requirements.

**Target Companies:**

* Gensler
* AECOM
* HOK
* Perkins&Will
* Turner Construction

**Key Buyer Persona:**
Director of Specifications, Principal Architect, Construction Administration Lead.

**Why They Buy:**
SuperDocs can automate the document comparison and prepare a reviewable response while maintaining the connection between the substitution request, specification, and related project documents. This can reduce repetitive manual review and help architects identify missing or conflicting requirements before approval.

# Summary

Across these industries, the common opportunity is not simply document generation. The higher-value problem is **maintaining consistency and traceability across collections of related documents while allowing humans to review and approve important changes**.

The strongest opportunities for SuperDocs are therefore workflows where:

* Multiple documents describe the same underlying business process.
* Information must be compared across documents.
* Documents change frequently.
* A small change can require updates in several related documents.
* Human approval is required before finalizing changes.
* The organization processes enough documents for manual work to become expensive.

This creates a repeatable commercial model for SuperDocs: **connect existing document workflows, identify the changes that matter, prepare precise edits, and keep the final decision with the responsible human.**
