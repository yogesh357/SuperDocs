2.1 CERAMIC TILE SPECIFICATIONS
A. Ceramic Floor Tile:
   1. Water Absorption: Less than 0.5% when tested in accordance with ASTM C373. (Clause 2.1.A.1)
   2. Dynamic Coefficient of Friction (DCOF): Greater than or equal to 0.42 when tested in wet conditions in accordance with ANSI A137.1. (Clause 2.1.A.2)
   3. Wear Rating: PEI IV or higher. (Clause 2.1.A.3)
   4. Manufacturer/Product: Subject to compliance with requirements, provide "GrandClassic" by SpecTile Inc. or approved equivalent. (Clause 2.1.A.4)
