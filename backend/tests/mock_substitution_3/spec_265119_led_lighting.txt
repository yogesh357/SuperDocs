SECTION 26 51 19 - LED INTERIOR LIGHTING

PART 2 - PRODUCTS

2.1 LIGHTING FIXTURE SPECIFICATIONS
B. Recessed LED Downlights:
   1. Luminous Efficacy: Greater than or equal to 120 lm/W when tested in accordance with IES LM-79. (Clause 2.1.B.1)
   2. Color Rendering Index (CRI): Greater than or equal to 90 Ra. (Clause 2.1.B.2)
   3. Rated Life (L70): Greater than or equal to 50,000 hours when tested in accordance with IES LM-80. (Clause 2.1.B.3)
   4. Manufacturer/Product: Subject to compliance with requirements, provide "AuraGlow" by LumenCorp Ltd. or approved equivalent. (Clause 2.1.B.4)
