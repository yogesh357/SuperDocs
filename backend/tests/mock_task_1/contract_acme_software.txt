MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
BETWEEN
ACME SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS AND GLOBALTECH INC.

1. Services and Rates
Acme Software Solutions agrees to provide consulting and engineering services. The primary personnel assigned to the work will be Developer roles. Hourly rates for Developer services are agreed at $150.00 per hour.

2. Payment Terms
Invoices shall be issued monthly. GlobalTech Inc. shall make payments within 30 days of receiving a valid invoice (Net 30 payment terms).

3. Statement of Work (SOW) Requirement
All project scopes must be authorized under a valid Statement of Work (SOW) executed by both parties.
